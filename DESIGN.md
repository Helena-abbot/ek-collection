## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern minimalist luxury tailored for high-end ethnic couture, bridging heritage craftsmanship and contemporary editorial design. It addresses an affluent, discerning global audience shopping for bespoke stitched and artisanal unstitched collections. 

The aesthetic is anchored in spacious layouts, restrained editorial pacing, razor-thin structural detailing, and tactile refinement. The visual atmosphere evokes high-fashion publishing rather than transactional retail, treating garments as wearable works of art. Clean, rectilinear frames, subtle champagne micro-accents, and deliberate whitespace foster an aura of poise, timelessness, and effortless prestige.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic undertones with graphic depth:

- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` Soft Ivory/Cream):** Serves as the primary canvas across all viewports. Eliminates clinical digital starkness, providing a tactile, silk-paper backdrop that showcases intricate weaves, embroidery, and zari work naturally.
- **Primary Foundation (`#222222` Deep Charcoal/Slate):** Employs deep charcoal instead of pure black for heavy structural blocks, sticky headers, and primary interactions to maintain high contrast with softer edges.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C5A059` Metallic Champagne Gold):** Applied selectively to primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and delicate borders. Used sparingly to preserve visual impact and avoid gaudy heaviness.
- **Tertiary Border & Divider Tone (`#E8E2D5` Warm Alabaster):** Low-contrast structural lines that partition editorial segments without breaking negative space.
- **Text & Foreground Spectrum:**
  - Primary Headlines & Critical Copy: `#1A1A1A` (Jet Black)
  - Secondary Metadata, Fabric Details & Breadcrumbs: `#666666` (Muted Slate)
  - Ghost Borders & Subtle Dividers: `#D4CEBF`

## Typography

Typography balances high-contrast editorial serifs with utilitarian sans-serif functional copy.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for collection titles, campaign mantras, marquee banners, and product names. Generous line heights maintain a relaxed, premium pace.
- **Body & Navigation (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides sharp legibility at smaller scales for fabric descriptions, custom tailoring specifications, and cart details.
- **Caps & Badging:** Categorical markers, badges (such as "UNSTITCHED", "PURE SILK", "HANDWOVEN"), and micro-labels use wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.15em`) set in uppercase to match haute-couture print conventions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous margins that keep focus on editorial visuals:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 40px gutters (`gutter-desktop`), 80px canvas margin (`margin-desktop`). Maximum content container bound at 1440px for editorial integrity.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters (`gutter`), 40px margins (`margin-tablet`).
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px outer margin (`margin`).

Layout blocks avoid cramped density. Vertical module distances utilize `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` to deliver generous breathing room between campaign lookbooks, product grids, and craft storytelling segments.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy physical drop shadows, relying instead on flat surface planes, crisp borders, and subtle luminous diffusion:

- **Borders over Shadows:** Depth and layer containment are achieved via 1px architectural lines using Warm Alabaster (`#E8E2D5`) or Metallic Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`).
- **Hover Transitions:** Product tiles and editorial cards rise without shifting borders; subtle zoom-ins on imagery (transform scale `1.03` with a 450ms cubic-bezier transition) add dimension.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Navbars:** Drawers (such as the tailoring customizer and mini-cart) and sticky navigation use Soft Ivory (`#FBF9F5`) with 92% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. An ultra-diffuse ambient depth anchor (`0 16px 40px rgba(34, 34, 34, 0.04)`) separates floating surfaces from underlying photography.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strictly sharp profile (`roundedness: 0`). 

Corners are crisp 90-degree right angles across buttons, input fields, image wrappers, flyouts, and modal dialogues. This architectural discipline reflects luxury editorial layouts, fine stationery, and structured garment geometry. Pill shapes and soft rounded corners are omitted entirely.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Champagne Luxe):** Solid Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`) background, Jet Black (`#1A1A1A`) text, zero border-radius. Padding: 16px vertical, 36px horizontal. Text: `label-navigation`. Hover transition: Deep Charcoal background (`#222222`) with Warm Ivory (`#FBF9F5`) text.
- **Secondary (Editorial Wire):** Transparent background with a 1px solid Deep Charcoal (`#222222`) border. Text: Jet Black (`#1A1A1A`). Hover transition: fills with Charcoal (`#222222`), shifting text to Ivory (`#FBF9F5`).
- **Text Action:** Clean, tracked uppercase label with a persistent 1px hairline gold underline set 4px beneath the baseline.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Tiles:** Sharp rectangular wrappers (aspect ratio 3:4 or 2:3 vertical portraits). Flush edges with no shadow. Bottom section includes title (`Playfair Display`), unstitched/stitched tag (`label-caps`), and price (`label-price`). Hover triggers subtle zoom on photography and reveals a bottom slide-up "Quick View / Tailor" bar.
- **Craft Storytelling Panels:** Asymmetrical 2-column modules pairing full-bleed portrait photography with fine 1px champagne-outlined text containers.

### Form Inputs & Selection
- **Input Fields:** Minimalist single bottom-border line (1px `#E8E2D5`) across text inputs. On focus, the line transitions smoothly to Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`). Text input is set in `body-md` with floating uppercase micro-labels.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square check boxes (16x16px) and diamond-oriented radios. Unchecked: 1px `#D4CEBF` outline. Checked: Solid `#222222` fill with a crisp `#FBF9F5` check indicator.

### Tags, Chips & Badges
- **Collection Badges:** Border-only chips (1px `#C5A059`), transparent background, padding: 4px 10px, uppercase `label-caps`. Used to distinguish fabric classifications: "Raw Silk", "Chanderi", "Organza", "Semi-Stitched".

### Specialized Luxury Ethnic Components
- **Tailoring Customizer Selector:** Segmented rectilinear toggle tabs distinguishing "Unstitched Fabric" vs. "Custom Stitched Suit". Selected state renders in solid `#222222` with `#FBF9F5` typography; unselected state retains `#E8E2D5` 1px border.
- **Measurement Drawer:** Architectural flyout sliding from the right, divided by crisp champagne hairlines for custom neck, sleeve, and hemline options.
